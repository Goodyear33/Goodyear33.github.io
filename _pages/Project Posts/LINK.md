LINK was created when I realized that I knew basically nothing about practical electronics and mechatronics and wanted to change that.

I love robotics so making a robotic manipulator seemed like a good crucible to forge an understanding of designing and implementing electromechanical systems. Now a way to control it. What better way to generate robotic arm inputs than by using a real human arm? All I knew is that it would be really awesome to see if I could pull it off...

LINK is a 6-DOF robotic manipulator controlled by human arm movements using an interface glove sending commands via bluetooth to actuators. It has a max reach of 75cm, it's driven by 5 servo motors and a stepper motor to achieve full workspace controllability and has actuation capbilities at its end effector to interact with surroundings.

I want to take this design further in the future by implementing a more realistic human hand in the end effector for increased finer manipulability, improving bandwidth for use outside ranges bluetooth can't reach and adding useful tools for assisting utility operations such as a flashlight, screwdriver, magnifier and 3rd-hand operability.
