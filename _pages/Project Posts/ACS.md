A rover equipped with an active suspension can provide many benefits for planetary missions and challenges. Namely these benefits include increasing stability and   during traveral and robotic arm manipulation (see Publications tab). I wanted to introduce these benefits to my Universities rover team by designing, manufacturing and integrating an active suspension into the planned rover mechanical drivetrain system.

I have been developing the active suspension project for the past 3 years which has resulted in two Preliminary Design Reviews (the slideshow of the latest is shown below) and one conceptual design review. Each year's design built upon the strengths of the previous to continuously improve functionality and performance. 

This is a project I have spent an excessive amount of time and effort on. I am incredibly passionate about the active suspension system and how it can significantly enhance a rovers handling and traversal capabilities!
