A rover equipped with an active suspension can provide many benefits for planetary missions and challenges. Namely these benefits include increasing stability and   during traveral and robotic arm manipulation (see Publications tab). I wanted to introduce these benefits to my Universities rover team by designing, manufacturing and integrating an active suspension into the planned rover mechanical system.
